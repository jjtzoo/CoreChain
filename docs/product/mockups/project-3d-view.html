A project page, with its holes and grades in 3D

For the resident / project manager, on the web. It shows where each hole runs, which intervals were sampled and what the laboratory returned. It shows the recorded evidence and interprets nothing.

The data is not real. The six holes are the synthetic Cordillera porphyry sample. The sample numbers and the Cu and Au values are illustrative, made up from each interval's logged mineral. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, hover a sample, and click a hole's label or a row on the right.

Traces follow each hole's planned azimuth and dip; there is no downhole survey. Depths are below the collar. Grid squares are 25 m.

Where it opens from

Project names on the team overview, and "See in 3D" on a hole's page, which opens this view with that hole selected.

What it leaves to the hole page

The full log, core boxes, photos and custody. "Open hole page" goes there; this view does not repeat it.

Not in this step

Colouring by rock type, a cut-off grade slider, a section view on the phone, and anything interpretive such as surfaces or grade shells.
