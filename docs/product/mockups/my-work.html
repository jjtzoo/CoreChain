My work: what I did, by date

A geologist opens one screen and sees the work they did on any day, week or range of dates: metres logged, samples, custody steps, dispatches. It comes from what is already on the phone, so it works with no signal, and it can be shared as a day report.

Hole names are real (Alberta Geological Survey data). All numbers, people and times are illustrative, made up so the screens have something to show.

The day report (what "Share" sends)

A plain-text summary that goes through the phone's share sheet (WhatsApp, Messenger, email), so a geologist can send it to a resident manager at the end of a shift without typing it.

How it is decided

  • By the date the work was done, not the date it was synced: an interval logged on Tuesday with no signal still counts on Tuesday.
  • Metres logged is the length of the intervals logged that day. Editing an old interval does not count as new metres.
  • Custody steps count by when the step happened (the time typed in the form), so a step recorded late lands on the right day.
  • Works offline. Everything is calculated from the phone's own data. It shows the same numbers the manager will later see for this geologist.
  • Private to the geologist. It is their own record. Nothing here is a score or a ranking.

Questions to answer with a geologist

  • Which number do you report at the end of a shift? Metres logged, samples, or something else?
  • Does your resident manager already ask for a daily or weekly summary? In what form?
  • Would you rather send the report as text, or as a PDF?
