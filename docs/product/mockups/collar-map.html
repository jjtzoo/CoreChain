Mockup · E15 collar map, step 1

Pick a hole from the project's map

Open a project first, as today. Its drillholes then show as a list or as a map of collars. Tap a collar to open the hole. The list stays, because most logging happens in the core shed, where position doesn't matter.

What this step does and doesn't do

  • It works with no signal: the collars are already on the phone, and GPS needs no data connection.
  • It has no background map (roads, terrain, satellite). The grid lines are the scale. An offline background map per project is step 2 (E15-2), which needs a map library, file hosting and a new APK.
  • Holes without a collar are never hidden; they are listed under "No location yet". A planned hole has no collar position in CoreChain yet. Planned collar positions, for finding new pads, are E15-3.
  • When you are more than 5 km from the collars (in the office, for example), the map says how far away they are instead of zooming out to fit you.
  • List or Map is remembered while the app is open; List is the default.
