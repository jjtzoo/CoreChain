# CoreChain discovery questionnaire — version 1

**Estimated completion time:** 5 minutes  
**Audience:** Geologists and professionals with drilling or core-handling experience  
**Purpose:** Understand the real workflow and its pain points. Do not include confidential project, company, client, or assay information.

## Form introduction

I am developing an early app concept called CoreChain for exploration and mining teams. It may support the workflow from drill-core receipt and logging through sampling, laboratory results, QA/QC, and reporting.

I am looking for practical feedback based on your experience in the Philippines or elsewhere. This is research only; no confidential information is needed. Your answers will help ensure the concept reflects actual field practice rather than assumptions.

- Exploration or project geologist

- Other
