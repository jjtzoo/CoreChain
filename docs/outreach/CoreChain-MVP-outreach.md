# CoreChain MVP feedback request

Hi Sir [Surname],

I’m currently developing CoreChain, an early MVP concept for improving the workflow from drill-core logging to sample tracking, laboratory results, QA/QC review, and reporting.

I would really value your feedback on whether the proposed workflow is practical for exploration and mining operations in the Philippines, and whether it aligns with the operational controls and reporting requirements teams actually need.

The MVP currently includes:

- Core logging: geological intervals, recovery, lithology, alteration, mineralisation, structures, and core photos.
- Sample and custody tracking: sample IDs, QR codes, sample preparation, dispatch, and handover history.
- Laboratory and QA/QC review: assay imports, standards, blanks, duplicates, and issue flags.
- Operational dashboard and reporting: drilling progress, pending samples, validated results, and traceable reports.
- Project controls: user roles, geological code lists, sampling protocols, and approval workflows.

My goal is to make the process more reliable and easier to manage—not to replace the geologist’s judgment or create additional field work.

May I send you the MVP mock-up and ask for your candid feedback on what is correct, what is missing, and what would not be useful in a Philippine mining setting?
